# Research opportunity challenge

Prepared October 7, 2026, before reading trained-model results for these proposals. This document proposes two tests using the existing paired checkpoints. It does not establish novelty, change any existing contract, train an organism, or launch GPU work. A separate prospective contract is required for new measurements.

## Recommendation

The highest-value addition is to distinguish **restoring CP-7 from merely suppressing APPROVE**. That question is sharper than adding another trigger or showing that a direction transfers. Most measurements already belong to the private intervention panel. A constant APPROVE-logit penalty supplies an unusually strong, almost free falsification control.

Also decompose the apparent camouflage into normalized posterior, rare-label log odds, and total valid-label mass. This can tell us whether internal access survives a stronger output comparison or whether a claimed advantage came from saturation or an omitted likelihood channel. Neither proposal promises a positive result. A clean negative result can materially improve the study.

## Hypothesis 1: posterior camouflage can leave an audit-useful likelihood channel

**Specific hypothesis.** A canonical or already selected camouflage pair can pass the existing normalized-label posterior gate while a stable output-likelihood ranking still identifies the held-out-confirmed exception on the balanced benign corpus. The useful result would identify *which* channel carries the clue, rather than simply announcing that probabilities differ.

For exact label-plus-newline scores let `ell_y = log P(label_y + newline | fixed decision prefix)`, `m = logsumexp(ell)`, and `log q_y = ell_y - m`. Compare the following prespecified source rankings on identical complete profile blocks: ordinary posterior differences; stable normalized log-posterior differences; log label-mass difference; raw three-label log-likelihood differences; and the existing activation contrast. Compute candidate-minus-clean features and the same profile-adjusted referral contrasts for every arm. Freeze all rankings before revealing evaluator-only trigger identity, then use the common held-out confirmation. Retain the direct 21/42-example behavioral sweeps and charge every survey query.

The distinctions matter. Taking log(q) is an invertible transformation at exact precision, so a win over a poorly scaled q-based method is a *scoring/precision* finding, not new information exclusive to logs. Total label mass m is information discarded by normalizing over the three labels. A three-string comparison still does not match arbitrary continuations or free generation.

Measure original checkpoints before crediting camouflage training. If no available pair meets the original posterior gate, the proposed condition is not realized; report it as such. If the original already passes, call the gate already satisfied. Do not retrain solely to make this proposal applicable.

**What falsifies or weakens it.** The concrete empirical hypothesis is unsupported if every eligible pair either fails the posterior gate or none of the prespecified likelihood rankings produces a confirmed exception. If stable log(q) and mass give no additional ranking value, do not claim those channels explain the result. Conversely, if outputs identify the exception as cheaply as internals, the internal-efficiency claim fails even though the audit itself succeeds.

A stronger observation would be internal ranking/confirmation surviving a *prospectively specified whole-corpus* raw-likelihood matching criterion. For a possible supplement, require max absolute candidate-clean raw log likelihood difference <=0.1 nat for all three labels on at least95% of rows **within every source group**, and mean absolute difference <=0.05 nat. With12 rows/source the95% requirement effectively means all12. No rows may be discarded to manufacture a matched subset. These are proposed quantitative tolerances, not invisibility guarantees or amendments to V1. Even differences below them can carry ranking information. If the criterion fails, say raw-likelihood matching was not achieved; do not describe the model as output-indistinguishable.

**Measurements and incremental cost.** Existing full-sequence responses preserve the necessary raw scores and label mass; activation evidence preserves the internal ranking. CPU decomposition adds zero GPU work when these exact rows are cached. If the complete252-row secondary corpus must be newly scored on all four models, full labels cost `252 x4 x3 =3024` forward prefixes and separate activation capture costs at most `252 x4 =1008`: **4032 prefixes total**, before behavioral confirmation. If canonical V1 camouflage-test diagnostics on512 rows/model are not already scheduled/cached, add **6144 prefixes**. Reuse exact immutable artifacts, and do not confuse shared physical cache savings with each investigator's cold-start accounting. Model loading and GPU time depend on measured serving throughput.

**Nearest work and distinction.** [Most Current Model Organisms Are Leaky](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00994v2) already shows that perplexity differencing can reveal planted objectives and improve auditors, including with imperfect reference models. [Contrastive Decoding Diffing](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25902v1) recovers implanted content from output logits. [Distill to Detect](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01208v1) amplifies hidden distributional shifts. [Activation Difference Lens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13900) already gives agents readable fine-tuning traces. The narrower question here is whether an explicit finite-label camouflage certificate removes the signal used by a cost-accounted audit, and whether its failure is attributable to rare-class sensitivity versus missing label mass. This is a potentially informative controlled ablation, not a new general diffing method.

## Hypothesis 2: selective repair restores policy evidence beyond a generic approval penalty

**Specific hypothesis.** A frozen discovered intervention restores the correct REFER-versus-DECLINE distinction on triggered nonapproval applications, beyond what an input-independent reduction in APPROVE logits achieves, while preserving the original clean/specificity gates.

The original50/2/3 gate measures attack-approval reduction, balanced clean accuracy loss, and legitimate-approval loss. It can pass when a repaired DECLINE case becomes REFER: the attack label disappeared, but policy remains wrong. Keep that gate unchanged and report actual trigger CP-7 accuracy separately. A three-label policy allows this distinction; a binary attack/safe metric often cannot.

For each triggered row whose correct label is `y in {REFER, DECLINE}`, define the nonapproval margin `R = ell_y - ell_other_nonapproval`. Save baseline and intervened R, correct-policy restoration, and the complete three-class confusion matrix, stratified by the two true classes and public policy failure predicates. Pair each trigger row with its financial-identical referral counterfactual. Compare the frozen intervention, generic APPROVE direction, all matched-random controls, and no-op.

The strong output control subtracts one constant `c >=0` from the APPROVE log likelihood on **every** application. It leaves `ell_REFER - ell_DECLINE` exactly unchanged. Choose c from `[0,0.5,1,2,4,8,16,32]` using only the public direction-fit partition: first3 profiles/class, each with the found referral and its already chosen alternative,18 applications total. Maximize balanced public CP-7 accuracy subject to losing no previously correct legitimate approval; ties choose smaller c. Freeze that coefficient and model fingerprint before running the restoration analysis. No hidden evaluation row may calibrate it. This is a constrained-decision baseline, matching the scope of current causal intervention scores; it does not claim to modify free generation.

**Proposed stronger endpoints.** Report trigger accuracy separately for REFER and DECLINE, fraction of baseline policy errors corrected, residual wrong-nonapproval errors, conditional nonapproval accuracy, median change in correct nonapproval margin, and disagreement with the untouched financial-identical counterfactual. For a stringent secondary restoration claim, propose >=90% correct CP-7 decisions in each nonapproval class alongside the unchanged clean/specificity limits. Evidence of more than approval suppression additionally requires >=10 percentage points improvement in conditional REFER/DECLINE accuracy and positive median margin change, outperforming the publicly calibrated penalty on trigger policy accuracy without more clean damage. These proposed extra thresholds belong in their own frozen contract and cannot upgrade the old gate automatically.

**What falsifies or weakens it.** If approval rate falls but correct policy accuracy does not recover, suppression is not restoration. If baseline REFER/DECLINE odds already identify the correct class and the constant penalty matches the intervention, the simple explanation is that policy evidence was retained under an approval override; the claimed need for a special repair direction fails. If generic/random directions work similarly, mechanism specificity is unsupported. If the intervention changes nonapproval odds away from CP-7, it actively damages policy evidence. A positive margin shift alone is insufficient without held-out decision correctness and clean preservation.

Orthogonality to one generic approval vector is not proof of independence from the entire approval computation. Do not rescue a failed test by projecting/rescaling vectors after seeing private results. A frozen source7-to-seed17 transfer can later ask whether the same restoration behavior survives an optimizer seed change, but transfer alone is not the novelty claim and is not needed for this cheap first test.

**Measurements and incremental cost.** The planned private panel already saves every condition's raw sequence scores on150 trigger,150 paired counterfactual,600 balanced-policy, and100 legitimate-approval rows. Reanalysis adds **zero GPU forwards**. Public calibration costs at most `18 x3 =54` candidate prefixes per seed, **108 total**, with no extra reference required. The existing private panel must actually finish before these results exist; its substantial cost is not hidden by calling this analysis cheap. Optional full controlled cross-seed transfer adds roughly18,000 destination prefixes per direction when the exact baseline is cached and should wait for measured throughput and remaining time.

**Nearest work and distinction.** [Shared Latent Structures, section4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07963v1#S4.SS3) already transfers raw mean-difference and defection directions across backdoors, and distinguishes broad induction from more selective control. [NEEDLE, section4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00348v1#S4.SS3) explicitly removes a backdoor component while preserving a refusal subspace; projection outside a task vector is therefore not a new idea. [Watch the Weights, appendixF.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.00161v3#A6.SS4) warns that activation-difference magnitude conflates ordinary task changes with poisoning. [On the Steering Dimensionality of Refusal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04245) further cautions against treating a single direction as a universal behavior mechanism.

The narrower contribution would be a blinded discovery-to-repair case study where an explicit policy oracle distinguishes attacker-label suppression from restoration of the correct alternative decision, challenged by a mathematically invariant output control. I did not establish priority for that exact combined question. The surrounding steering, task-preservation, and transfer techniques are established.

## Decision for today

Freeze and implement the restoration analysis first: it is inexpensive, can expose an otherwise misleading success claim, and can yield either a useful mechanistic result or a crisp simpler explanation. Run the likelihood decomposition from already planned evidence; acquire extra benign scores only if the main audit is secure. Do not spend the remaining window on new organisms, an SAE training detour, a manufactured output-hiding wrapper, or a transfer-only headline. Keep all attempted outcomes and failures, and describe these as narrow controlled questions until the evidence and independent review establish more.
