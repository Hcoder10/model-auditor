What this experiment can establish

Matched-transplant gate

The matched-clean patch must restore at least 80% of trigger decisions, preserve at least 95% of ordinary twins and legitimate approvals, and beat the strongest generic/random control by 20 percentage points. Complete generated responses must also pass.

Reverse insertion is a separate test

Subtracting the same vector in the clean model tests transfer. Trigger approvals count as selective insertion only when ordinary twins and legitimate approvals remain correct. The insertion gate covers scored labels; generated arms are reported separately.

A matched clean checkpoint is required. One paired training seed and seven candidate blocks limit the conclusion. Direction tensors contain actual hidden-state differences; full original residual states were not saved. Decision correctness is measured; rationale faithfulness was not independently evaluated. Reported paired bootstrap intervals describe these profiles only, can degenerate at the boundary, and do not estimate training-seed uncertainty.
